Exclusive Lead Tracker · 3 UI proposals

Static mockups of Joanna Paclibare's page with sample data. Each has the metrics, daily mix, Nov 6 carry-over, Nov 1–4 history, lead table, settings and sidebar entry. Pick one and the build gets planned.

Proposal 1 · KPI cards on top, table below

Month/Day toggle on four target cards, then mix, carry-over and Nov 1–4 history, then the lead table. Settings open in a modal.

Pros: closest to the Dashboard cards, easy to scan. Cons: the lead table sits far down the page.

Proposal 2 · Sticky metrics side panel

Targets, progress and Nov 6 mix stay in a right-hand panel while she scrolls the table. Tabs for Leads, Tomorrow and History. Settings open in a drawer.

Pros: targets always visible while working leads. Cons: table is narrower, and the panel stacks above it on small screens.

Proposal 3 · Daily timeline with tabs

A November day strip (250 a day, Nov 1–4 at 100, tomorrow marked), progress bars and the mix. Leads, history and settings are separate tabs.

Pros: best view of the month and the carry-over. Cons: leads are one click away, so most to build.

Assumptions to confirm: 54 confirmed orders is a monthly target (about 2 a day); 250 a day × 28 days = 7,000, and the daily repeat goal is 50 (20% of 250). Sample names, products and numbers are made up.
