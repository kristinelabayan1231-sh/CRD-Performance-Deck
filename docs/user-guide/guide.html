CRD Performance Deck

User Guide

Purpose

CRD Performance Deck turns delivered orders into daily follow-up leads for the CRA team. It assigns leads automatically, tracks every call outcome, carries unfinished work to the next day, and shows team performance at a glance.

Tech stack

Laravel 13 (PHP 8.5)MySQL 9Tailwind CSS 4 ViteBlade + JavaScriptGoogle Sign-in (OAuth) Logistics APIPancake POS / Pages API

Dashboard

Team performance at a glance, for today, this week or this month.

Everyone · CRAs see only their own numbers
  1. 1Switch period: Today / Week / Month.
  2. 2Leads, Processed %, Converted %, Went cold %, each compared with the previous period (▲ / ▼).
  3. 3Daily trend: processed vs. unprocessed.
  4. 4Customer tags: Hot, Cold, Warm, High value.

Good to know

  • Processed = a status was set. Converted = Repeat Purchase "Yes", or "No" with feedback PURCHASED.
  • Green arrows are good changes; red arrows need attention.

Segmentation Tracker — Daily

Customers whose product runs out today become leads. Each CRA gets 70 a day, returning customers first, and extras are split evenly.

CRAs see their own leads · Supervisors & admins see all

Overview

  1. 1Daily and Weekly Segmentation tabs.
  2. 2Leads sync automatically every hour. Sync now is for managers.
  3. 3Filters: month, date, CRA, type, status. Today resets them.
  4. 4Live totals: Leads, CRD Leads, New Customers, Per CRA, Status Updated, Conversion.

Leads table

  1. 1Columns: show or hide optional columns.
  2. 2Customer name. Hover it to add a note.
  3. 3Days since delivered.

Tracking fields

  1. 1Status: the call outcome.
  2. 2Repeat Purchase? Yes / No / Reserve.
  3. 3Customer Tagging: Hot, Warm, Cold, High Value…
  4. 4Optional columns (green headers).

How to use

  1. Call the customer, then pick a Status. It saves instantly, and the field flashes green when saved.
  2. Fill in Repeat Purchase, Tagging, Date / Time of Contact, Feedback and Callback Date.

Per CRA

  1. 1Click the Per CRA tile to see each CRA's assigned leads. Uneven counts are flagged.

Show / hide columns

  1. 1Tick or untick columns. Your choice is remembered. "Coming soon" columns aren't available yet.

Notes

  1. 1A black corner means the customer has a note.
  2. 2Hover to read it, with who wrote it and when.

How to use

  1. Hover a customer name and click the pencil.
  2. Type the note and click Save. Use Delete note to remove it.

Carry-over & Transfers

These customers move to the next day with the same CRA: no status, PJR, Repeat Purchase or Inactive. Carry-over stops once the status changes to Active, Busy/Callback, Reminders/FFUP or Blocked.

Carry-over list

  1. 1Carry-over section and count, below today's leads.
  2. 2Reason and date, e.g. "Unprocessed since Sep 29".
  3. 3Transfer one customer (Supervisors).
  4. 4Tick several, then Transfer selected.

Transferring a lead

  1. 1Each CRA's current load: today's leads and carry-over. Pick one.

Confirm the transfer

  1. 1Review the summary.
  2. 2Confirm transfer. It's recorded and shows "from [CRA]".

  3. 3Change role.

Settings — Products

Groups product name variants under one product, e.g. "Pterygium Drops" and "Pterygium Eye Drops" → Pterygium.

  1. 1Add a product (its name is the keyword) and optional extra spellings.
  2. 2Also matches: other spellings, comma-separated.

Good to know

  • Matching ignores capitals and spaces: "Clearsight" also matches "Clear Sight 3.0".
  • Existing leads re-group automatically after you save.

Glossary

LeadA customer whose product is estimated to run out today (from the Logistics API).
CRD LeadA returning customer (more than one delivered order).
New CustomerA first-time buyer.
Processed / HandledA status has been set.
Carry-overNo status, PJR, Repeat Purchase or Inactive. Moves to the next day.
ConvertedRepeat Purchase "Yes", or "No" with feedback PURCHASED.
Recommended Replenishment DayEst. Out of Stock minus 7 days.
